How discounts work
  • A product's own discount (set here, in the product form, or with the Discount columns in Excel) always wins while it is running.
  • Otherwise, if several sales cover the same product, the customer gets the biggest saving. Discounts never stack.
  • "Never discount" on a product keeps it at full price during every sale.
  • Where a visitor can't see prices (login / contact for price), they still see the badge — never the numbers. Inquiries and the cart record the discounted price the customer was shown.
  • Dates use your computer's clock ({{ tzNote }}). A sale with no end date runs until you pause or delete it.
