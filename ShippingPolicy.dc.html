Shipping and Delivery Policy

Last updated: August 26, 2026

On this page
1. Overview 2. Shipping Methods 3. Delivery Timelines 4. International Shipping & Customs 5. Risk of Loss & Insurance 6. Order Tracking 7. Contact Us

1. Overview

Given the value and nature of certified diamonds, gemstones and fine jewelry, we do not use an automated checkout-and-fulfillment pipeline. Once your purchase is confirmed with our sales desk (see our Payment Policy), shipping is arranged individually for your order by your account contact.

2. Shipping Methods

We ship using insured, trackable couriers appropriate to the item's value, weight, and destination. Because loose certified stones, parcels, and finished jewelry each carry different handling requirements, the specific courier and service level for your order will be confirmed by your account contact rather than chosen at checkout.

3. Delivery Timelines

Delivery time depends on the item's availability, whether any customization or resizing is required, and your destination. Your account contact will give you an estimated timeline when your order is confirmed, and will update you if anything changes.

4. International Shipping & Customs

We serve trade partners and collectors across many countries. For international orders, you are responsible for any import duties, taxes, or customs fees charged by your country on arrival — these are set by your local authorities and are outside our control. We declare the contents and value of every shipment accurately in line with destination-country requirements.

5. Risk of Loss & Insurance

Shipments are insured for their full declared value while in transit. Responsibility for the item transfers to you once it is delivered and signed for at the address you provided; please inspect your shipment promptly on arrival and see our Refund and Return Policy for what to do if anything arrives damaged or incorrect.

6. Order Tracking

Because shipping is arranged per order rather than through an automated storefront, tracking details are provided directly by your account contact once your order ships, rather than through a self-service tracking page on this Site.

7. Contact Us

Questions about the shipping of a specific order should go to your ONCD account contact directly. General questions about this Policy can be sent via our Contact page, or to {{ footerEmail }}.
